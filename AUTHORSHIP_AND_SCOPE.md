# Authorship and project scope

Music Cultural Genealogy is an independent project by **@ANDreaLiuz**. The author states that the research framing, collection and analysis workflow, computational experiments and interpretation were conducted individually. No other student or faculty researcher co-developed this project. Academic affiliation is background, not a claim of institutional sponsorship or endorsement.

OpenL3, librosa, Leiden community detection, Ward hierarchical clustering and other established tools and algorithms retain their upstream authorship. The research audio, features and source implementations remain private pending separate review of publication and redistribution rights. The standalone synthetic demo and portfolio presentation materials were made with AI assistance, as documented in the README.

No actual musical genealogy, listener preference migration or historical cultural descent is claimed as validated. No general reuse license has been granted for original repository materials.
